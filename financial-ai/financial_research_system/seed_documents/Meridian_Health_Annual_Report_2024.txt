SECTION 1: BUSINESS OVERVIEW
Meridian Health Systems Inc. ("Meridian") manufactures diagnostic medical
devices used in hospitals and outpatient clinics. The Company sells through a
direct sales force in the United States and through distributors internationally.

SECTION 2: MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Fiscal Year 2024 was a challenging year. Revenue declined slightly as hospital
capital equipment budgets tightened. Margins compressed due to component cost
inflation and one-time charges related to a product recall in the second
quarter. The Company also drew down an additional term loan to maintain
liquidity during the recall remediation period.

SECTION 4: RISK FACTORS AND AUDITOR NOTES
The independent auditor issued an unqualified opinion but included an
emphasis-of-matter paragraph related to an ongoing product liability lawsuit
connected to the Q2 FY2024 recall of the M-200 diagnostic scanner line. The
Company recorded a $34.50 million litigation reserve during the year.
Management disclosed a material weakness in internal controls over inventory
valuation that was identified during the recall investigation and is in the
process of remediation. Days sales outstanding increased from 51 to 66 days.
