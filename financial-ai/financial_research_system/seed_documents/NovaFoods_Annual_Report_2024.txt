SECTION 1: BUSINESS OVERVIEW
Nova Foods Corporation ("Nova Foods") manufactures and distributes packaged
snack foods across North America and Europe. The Company operates three
manufacturing plants and sells through both retail and e-commerce channels.

SECTION 2: MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Fiscal Year 2024 revenue grew modestly as volume growth was largely offset by
promotional discounting in a competitive pricing environment. Raw material costs
(notably cooking oil and packaging) declined from FY2023 peaks, supporting a
modest gross margin recovery. The Company reduced total debt during the year by
repaying a portion of its revolving credit facility.
