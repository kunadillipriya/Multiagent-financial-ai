SECTION 1: BUSINESS OVERVIEW
Aurora Technologies Inc. ("Aurora", "the Company") designs and sells cloud-based
data analytics software for mid-market enterprises. The Company operates in two
segments: Software Subscriptions and Professional Services. Aurora was founded in
2011 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas.

SECTION 2: MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS
Fiscal Year 2024 was a year of continued growth alongside rising cost pressure.
Subscription revenue grew steadily, driven by expansion within existing enterprise
accounts. However, the Company saw a meaningful increase in borrowing to fund a
new data center build-out, and gross margin contracted slightly compared to the
prior year due to higher cloud infrastructure costs.

SECTION 4: RISK FACTORS AND AUDITOR NOTES
The Company's independent auditor issued an unqualified (clean) opinion for FY2024,
but the Notes to Financial Statements include an emphasis-of-matter paragraph
regarding a material increase in long-term debt used to finance the new data
center, and a covenant requiring the Company to maintain a debt-to-equity ratio
below 1.00. Management notes that a continued rise in interest expense could
pressure net income in future periods. Accounts receivable days outstanding
increased from 42 days to 58 days year over year, which management attributes to
extended payment terms offered to two large enterprise customers.
